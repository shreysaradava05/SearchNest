About SearchNest

SearchNest is a simple search page made as a college project. It uses only HTML and CSS, with no JavaScript.

How it works

  1. You type a word in the search box.
  2. You click Google, DuckDuckGo or Bing.
  3. The form sends your word to that search engine and shows the results in a new tab.

Features

  • Works with 3 search engines
  • Quick links to popular sites
  • Works on mobile and computer
  • SEO tags for better search ranking
